# Scripter

This folder is the working base for creating Romanian cabana showreel scripts that are at least 80% ready before a client shoot.

The intended workflow is simple:

1. Input cabana information: location, amenities, surroundings, booking details, target guest, and any client notes.
2. Generate 5 to 10 Romanian short-form showreel scripts at a time.
3. Human review should mainly correct facts, client preferences, and small Romanian polish, not rewrite the whole thing.

## Main Use Case

The main product is a short-form showreel script for cabane.

These videos should show:

- the cabana itself
- arrival and exterior
- rooms and layout
- amenities
- surrounding nature or nearby attractions
- practical guest details
- the feeling of staying there
- a clear booking CTA

The goal is not generic real estate copy. The goal is a natural Romanian spoken script that gives the shoot a strong direction without turning the output into a production checklist.

## Reference Material

The Romanian videos are used mainly to learn natural Romanian rhythm, phrasing, and speech flow.

The English videos are used for general scripting structure, visual direction, pacing, hooks, and shot logic. They should not influence Romanian wording too directly.

Important: the transcriptions are machine-generated. They are useful for cadence and structure, but Romanian text still needs judgment. Do not blindly copy misheard words, brand names, prices, or odd phrasing from transcripts.

## Romanian Tone Of Voice

The Romanian voice should feel:

- natural
- warm
- spoken, not written
- visual
- concrete
- calm but engaging
- lightly cinematic when the property supports it
- clear enough for social media
- specific enough to help the shoot

The script should sound like a good host walking someone through a place, not like a brochure, not like a translated English ad, and not like a formal property listing.

Preferred energy:

- relaxed confidence
- "hai sa vezi" curiosity
- simple sensory details
- practical usefulness
- emotional but not cheesy

## Romanian Speech Patterns To Use

Use phrases like:

- "La mai putin de doua ore de Bucuresti..."
- "Locul asta e gandit pentru..."
- "Aici ai..."
- "In interior gasesti..."
- "Te trezesti cu privelistea asta..."
- "E genul de loc in care..."
- "Daca vii cu familia..."
- "Daca vrei un weekend cu prietenii..."
- "Afara ai..."
- "Iar partea cea mai buna e..."
- "Pentru date libere, trimite-ne mesaj."
- "Scrie-ne si iti spunem ce variante sunt disponibile."

Good Romanian flow usually follows this shape:

```text
[unde suntem] + [de ce conteaza] + [ce vezi] + [pentru cine e] + [ce trebuie sa faci]
```

Example:

```text
Suntem la marginea padurii, intr-o cabana gandita pentru weekenduri linistite.
Jos ai livingul cu semineu si bucatarie complet utilata, iar sus sunt dormitoarele, cu vedere spre munte.
Afara ai terasa, gratar si ciubar, adica exact locul in care o sa ramai dupa apus.
Pentru date libere, trimite-ne mesaj.
```

## Romanian Patterns To Avoid

Avoid:

- mirror-translated English
- formal phrases that sound like real estate listings
- overusing "beneficiaza de"
- overusing "dispune de"
- "experimenteaza luxul" unless the client is explicitly luxury-formal
- "o oaza de relaxare unica"
- "facilitati premium" as filler
- long lists of amenities without emotion or visual context
- starting every sentence with "avem"
- exaggerated hype that does not match the place
- too many adjectives in a row
- stiff corporate Romanian
- lazy AI Romanian
- "vibe de vacanta" as filler
- "instant intri in modul de vacanta"
- "sejur confortabil"
- "momente de rasfat"
- "gasesti locul asta"
- mixing English words like "view", "party", "open space" unless they are clearly natural for that client's audience

Better replacements:

- Instead of "beneficiaza de ciubar si terasa", write "afara ai ciubarul si terasa, locul perfect dupa apus."
- Instead of "dispune de trei dormitoare", write "sunt trei dormitoare, asa ca merge bine pentru familie sau un grup de prieteni."
- Instead of "experimenteaza o sedere unica", write "e genul de loc in care vii pentru doua zile de liniste."
- Instead of "vibe de vacanta imediat", write "intri repede in ritmul ala de weekend in care nu te mai grabesti."
- Instead of "momente de rasfat", write "seri lungi afara, dupa gratar, cu focul aprins."

## Cabana Showreel Structure

Use this structure as the default:

1. Hook
   Start with the strongest reason to watch: location, view, feeling, privacy, capacity, unusual amenity, or escape from the city.

2. Arrival / exterior
   Show road, entrance, facade, forest, mountain, lake, yard, terrace, lights, smoke, snow, sunrise, or sunset.

3. Interior comfort
   Move through living, fireplace, sofa, kitchen, dining, windows, textures, and natural light.

4. Sleeping and practical proof
   Mention bedrooms, capacity, bathrooms, heating, parking, pet policy, kids, groups, or remote work only when relevant.

5. Amenities
   Show ciubar, sauna, gratar, foisor, terrace, fire pit, jacuzzi, pool, playground, hammock, bikes, games, or anything distinctive.

6. Surroundings
   Mention nearby trails, forest, lake, river, ski slope, town, restaurant, viewpoint, or drive time.

7. Ideal use case
   Say who it is for: couples, families, friends, retreats, remote work, birthdays, quiet weekends.

8. CTA
   End with booking availability, DM, call, website, or ask-for-dates action.

## Script Output Format

For each generated script, include only:

- 3 hook options
- 1 final Romanian spoken script with natural line breaks

Do not include shot suggestions, voiceover labels, b-roll lists, overlay text, client-check questions, or production sections in the final app output.

For batches of 5 to 10 scripts, each script should have a distinct angle. Do not generate the same structure with swapped words.

Useful angles:

- weekend escape
- family trip
- friends group
- romantic stay
- luxury comfort
- nature and quiet
- amenities-first
- view-first
- close-to-city
- seasonal: winter, summer, autumn, rainy weekend

## Quality Bar

A script is 80% ready when:

- the Romanian sounds natural out loud
- every sentence has a visual reason to exist
- the first 3 seconds have a clear hook
- the cabana's strongest selling point appears early
- amenities are turned into reasons to care
- surroundings are included when they strengthen the story
- the CTA is clear
- the script can be edited like a note/document after generation
- the client can fact-check details without rewriting the whole thing

## Information Needed Before The First Test Script

To generate the first strong test script, provide:

- cabana name
- location
- drive time from major nearby cities, especially Bucuresti or Cluj if relevant
- capacity: number of guests
- bedrooms and bathrooms
- main amenities: ciubar, sauna, jacuzzi, fireplace, gratar, terrace, pool, playground, etc.
- surroundings: forest, mountain, lake, river, trails, ski slope, viewpoints, restaurants, attractions
- strongest visual feature
- target guest: couples, families, friends, corporate retreats, premium travelers, etc.
- booking CTA: phone, Instagram DM, website, Airbnb, WhatsApp
- tone preference: calm cinematic, warm family, premium, playful, direct sales
- any facts that must be mentioned
- any words or claims to avoid
- season of the shoot or campaign
- desired video length: 20s, 30s, 45s, 60s

Optional but useful:

- current listing link
- photos or video clips
- price range or starting price
- availability urgency
- pet policy
- check-in/check-out details
- unique story of the owner or property
- common objections guests have
