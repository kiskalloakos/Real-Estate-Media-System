# Realty Media Script System

Goal: get each client shoot to at least 80% script-ready before the team arrives on site.

## Inputs Needed Before Writing

Collect these before generating a script:

- Property type: cabana, apartment, house, development, commercial space
- Location and travel context: exact area, nearby landmarks, drive time, access
- Target guest or buyer: family, couples, investors, remote workers, city escape, luxury buyer
- Top 3 emotional hooks: privacy, view, comfort, novelty, investment, convenience, lifestyle
- Top 5 factual hooks: rooms, capacity, price, surface, amenities, parking, heating, dates, commission
- Must-show visuals: hero exterior, arrival, view, kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom, fireplace, ciubar, terrace, details
- Offer and CTA: book, DM, call, schedule viewing, ask for brochure, check availability
- Romanian voice direction: warm, natural, direct, not translated from English

## Output Standard

Every draft should include:

- Hook: 1 sentence that names the strongest reason to keep watching
- Movement plan: where the camera starts, moves, and ends
- Scene beats: 6 to 10 short sections, each tied to a visual
- Voiceover: natural spoken Romanian or English, not brochure text
- On-screen text: 3 to 6 short overlays
- CTA: clear next step with urgency or qualification
- Optional alternates: one more emotional hook and one more factual hook

## Best Structure For Cabana / Showreel

1. Arrival hook
   Start with the promise, not the inventory.
   Example: "La mai putin de doua ore de oras, locul asta se simte ca o pauza reala."

2. Establish the escape
   Show exterior, landscape, road, terrace, view, smoke/fire/light.

3. Walk into comfort
   Move inside naturally: living, fireplace, kitchen, dining.

4. Prove it works for real guests
   Mention bedrooms, capacity, bathrooms, heating, parking, kids, groups, pets only if relevant.

5. Add sensory details
   Ciubar, gratar, cafea dimineata, lumina, liniste, ploaie, zapada, apus, lemn, pat, textile.

6. Name the ideal use case
   "Pentru un weekend cu familia", "pentru un grup de prieteni", "pentru doua zile fara notificari".

7. Close with action
   Ask for booking/viewing/contact in one clean sentence.

## Romanian Phrasing Guardrails

Prefer:

- "la mai putin de doua ore de Bucuresti"
- "locul asta"
- "in weekend"
- "cu tot ce ai nevoie"
- "te trezesti cu privelistea asta"
- "e gandita pentru"
- "ai aici"
- "se simte"
- "daca vrei sa"
- "scrie-ne pentru disponibilitate"

Avoid:

- English mirror phrasing like "experimenteaza luxul", unless the brand is intentionally luxury-formal
- Overwritten claims like "o oaza de relaxare unica"
- Robotic real estate phrasing like "beneficiaza de facilitati premium"
- Too many adjectives in a row
- Starting every line with "avem"
- Long sentences that list all features before giving the viewer a reason to care

## Romanian Flow Formula

Use this pattern for natural speech:

`[where we are] + [why it matters] + [what you see] + [who it is for] + [what to do next]`

Example:

"Suntem la marginea padurii, intr-o cabana facuta pentru weekenduri linistite. Jos ai livingul cu semineu si bucatarie complet utilata, iar sus sunt doua dormitoare cu vedere spre munte. E genul de loc in care vii cu familia sau cu prietenii si nu mai simti nevoia sa pleci. Pentru date libere, trimite-ne mesaj."

## Quality Checklist

- The first sentence would make sense even with the sound off when paired with the opening shot
- The script names the audience clearly
- Feature lists are converted into benefits
- Romanian sounds like a person talking, not translated ad copy
- Every spoken beat has a visual reason to exist
- The CTA is specific
- The script leaves room for natural pauses and camera movement
- The client can correct facts without rewriting the whole concept
