# Reference Learnings

These videos are useful for structure, pacing, hooks, and spoken Romanian flow. The transcripts are machine-generated, so Romanian wording still needs human polish before it becomes client-facing copy.

## What Works Across The References

- Open with a strong situational promise: distance from the city, price, availability, hidden problem, or unique feature.
- Move quickly from hook to proof. Good videos do not spend long setting context.
- Tie each sentence to a visible thing: view, room, fixture, price, terrace, ciubar, kitchen, bedroom, paperwork, inspection.
- Speak like a person walking through the space, not like a property brochure.
- Repeat the most important commercial detail near the end: price, availability, capacity, commission, discount, booking CTA.
- Use practical details to build trust: what is included, who it fits, how many people, exact location, exact price, exact next step.

## Patterns Worth Reusing

- "Am gasit..." hook: good for discovery-style reels.
- "La mai putin de..." hook: good for cabane and weekend escapes.
- "Aici poate fi..." hook: good for apartments and buyer imagination.
- "Daca vrei..." CTA: natural and direct in Romanian.
- "Hai sa vezi..." transition: useful when the camera is actively moving.
- "Pentru..." audience framing: family, friends, investors, couples, remote workers.

## What To Avoid For Our Cabana Showreel Direction

- Overloading the script with every feature in the first 10 seconds.
- Shouting or repeating "fratilor" unless the client's brand intentionally wants that energy.
- Listing rooms without turning them into a feeling or use case.
- Romanian that sounds like English ad copy translated line by line.
- Generic luxury language when the real appeal is warmth, quiet, family, nature, or convenience.

## Romanian Cleanup Notes

Whisper still mishears some Romanian, especially fast speech and brand/location words. Common cleanup areas:

- "weekend" may appear as broken words.
- "ciubar" may appear with inconsistent spelling.
- "priveliște" may be misheard.
- "dormitoare", "canapea", "bucatarie", "baie proprie" can need correction.
- Brand names and place names need manual verification.
- Prices and numbers must be checked against the client brief.

## Preferred Cabana Voice

Natural, calm, concrete:

"Suntem la marginea padurii, intr-o cabana gandita pentru weekenduri linistite. Jos ai livingul cu semineu si bucatarie complet utilata, iar sus sunt dormitoarele, cu vedere spre munte. Afara ai terasa, gratar si ciubar, adica exact locul in care o sa ramai dupa apus. Pentru date libere, trimite-ne mesaj."

## Production Rule

Use the transcripts to learn rhythm. Use the client brief to decide facts. Use the script system to shape the final shoot plan.
